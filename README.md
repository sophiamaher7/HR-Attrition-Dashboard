# HR-Attrition-Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition and identify patterns across demographic and employment-related factors.

## Overview

Employee attrition can have a significant impact on organizational performance, workforce stability, and recruitment costs. This project analyzes HR data to provide a clear overview of employee turnover and highlight patterns that may help support data-driven HR decision-making.

The dashboard allows users to explore attrition across different dimensions, including department, gender, age, marital status, years at the company, overtime, and time since the last promotion.

## Analysis

The dashboard provides analysis across the following areas:

- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Gender
- Attrition by Age
- Attrition by Marital Status
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Attrition by Overtime
- Attrition by Time Since Last Promotion

Interactive slicers allow users to filter the dashboard by:

- Department
- Marital Status
- Gender
- Years at the Company

## Key Insights

- The overall attrition rate is 16.82%, with 492 employees leaving out of a total workforce of 2,925 employees.
- Attrition varies across departments, indicating differences in employee retention between business areas.
- Employees with less than one year at the company have the highest attrition rate.
- Employees working overtime show a higher attrition rate than those who do not work overtime.
- Attrition patterns also vary across age groups, marital status, and promotion history.
